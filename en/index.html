The problem

The same uncertainties every year

Treating too early is a waste. Treating too late can cost you a whole harvest. Today many of these decisions are made by calendar, out of habit, or by watching the sky, with margins of error that weigh on your wallet and the environment.

Blind treatments

Diseases like downy mildew and powdery mildew are often fought "by calendar" rather than on real data. Result? Unnecessary or late treatments, higher costs and more environmental impact.

Irrigation by guesswork

Without data on soil moisture and evapotranspiration, you irrigate too much, too little, or at the wrong time, with direct effects on product quality.

Fragmented or missing data

Those who have a weather station end up with numbers to interpret on their own. Those who don't rely on their experience. With the right data, both make better decisions.

Silent pests

Olive fruit fly, vine moth, pear psylla: insects move before you notice them. Without continuous monitoring, you realize there's an infestation when the damage is already done.

Frosts and temperature swings

A few degrees below zero during flowering can wipe out a whole harvest. Thermal stress and late frosts strike silently, and without real-time alerts there's no margin to intervene.

The solution

A sentinel in the field
It measures, predicts, and tells you when to act

  • Microclimate and soil sensors Temperature, air and soil humidity, leaf wetness, rain, wind, radiation and much more. All in a single station, powered by a solar panel.
  • Integrated predictive models Data is analyzed by agronomic and machine learning models that estimate phytosanitary risk, water stress, and phenological stage — not just today, but over the next 48-72 hours.
  • Actionable alerts on your smartphone No graphs to interpret, just clear notifications. You decide, with the right information.
  • Designed to grow Born for the vineyard, designed to expand: QuietField's modular architecture adapts to any crop.
