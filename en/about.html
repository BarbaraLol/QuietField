Visionary & Founder

Barbara Loletti: Where Land meets Code.

Computer engineer and data scientist, with a master's specialisation in AI between Trieste and Alicante, Barbara combines analytical rigour with strategic vision. Her expertise spans advanced statistical modelling and embedded systems, with a vertical focus on agritech innovation.

QuietField was born from the conviction that intelligent monitoring should be a democratic opportunity. The goal is to provide every producer with accessible and reliable tools capable of transforming raw data into strategic insights for field management.

Her approach puts people first: working closely with those who live the land every day, Barbara develops solutions that start from real problems, not just the potential of technology.
