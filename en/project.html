The Project

We're building QuietField
one step at a time

We don't start from a finished product, but from a real problem: we want every decision in the field to be based on real data, not on the calendar.

Where we are

A phased journey

QuietField is born from the meeting between agronomy and data science. We proceed in phases, validating each step before moving forward because we want it to really work in the field, not just on paper.

01

Research & Design

Study of relevant agronomic parameters and comparison with agronomists and researchers in the sector.

In progress 02

Prototyping

03

Field validation

Installation in the fields of the first partner producers, to measure real usefulness on the ground.

04

Crop expansion

Adaptation of the platform to new crops and production contexts.

The vision

One platform, every crop

Every crop has its challenges: a late treatment, mistimed irrigation, a pest that arrives silently. Decisions that weigh on the harvest and the environment.

We're building an architecture that doesn't stop at one plant: microclimate, soil, predictive models, clear alerts. An approach designed to adapt to vineyards, olive groves, orchards and beyond.

The goal remains the same: to make intelligent field monitoring accessible to every producer, not just those with large surfaces or large budgets.

Who it's for

We design with you, not for you

The project is in the initial development phase. We're looking for anyone who works the land, studies it or wants to invest in its future, and is willing to contribute their experience to build a tool that's truly useful in the field.

🌾 Growers

Production realities linked to the land, in every part of the world and on every scale, who want to transform data into better choices to reduce waste and enhance their work.

🔬 Researchers and agronomists

Professionals and academics in agronomy, plant pathology, entomology or precision farming interested in research and development collaborations.

🌱 Investors and partners

Those who believe that accessible precision agriculture is a concrete opportunity and want to contribute to making it a reality, supporting the development and dissemination of the tool.
