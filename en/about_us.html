Who's behind it

I'm Barbara Loletti

Founder of QuietField

Computer engineer and data scientist, with a master's degree in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence between the University of Trieste and the Universidad de Alicante. My skills range from statistical modeling to embedded systems, with concrete application in the agricultural sector.

I founded QuietField because I believe that intelligent field monitoring should not be a luxury reserved for a few. The goal is to give every producer an accessible and reliable tool that transforms field data into concretely useful information.

I work closely with those who live in the field every day: to build something truly useful, you need to start from those who face these problems every day, not just from technology.
